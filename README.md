Minecraft Skript plugin and scripts largely inspired by RootCellar's work: https://github.com/RootCellar

These are scripts designed to give Minecraft an RPG feel with elements like classes, levels, advanced monsters, spells, and other special features typically found in RPG games. This is the version I have upgraded to a more modern version of Minecraft and Skript with a variety of other plugins.

After cooperating on the scripts together, I will now manage and update them myself as of his passing.
